## 1. Executive Brand Narrative & Positioning

### 1.1 The Dual-Competency Advantage
Most undergraduate candidates in Computer Science fall into one of two polarized categories:
1. **The Model Experimenter:** Writes machine learning notebooks, understands metrics, but struggles to deploy models, package APIs, or construct responsive interfaces.
2. **The Conventional Web Developer:** Builds responsive React frontends and CRUD backends, but treats artificial intelligence as an unfamiliar black box.

**Sri Harsha’s Positioning:**
Sri Harsha represents a high-value hybrid profile: **an emerging AI/ML Engineer who builds complete, production-ready software systems**. He combines formal academic foundations in machine learning algorithms, deep learning architectures, and computer vision with practical full-stack execution (React.js, Flask, Node.js, relational databases). His engineering philosophy centers on taking intelligent models out of research sandboxes and operationalizing them in robust, user-centric software.
